# Lipman review portal

[Open the portal](https://satviktri.github.io/lipman-portal/)

2027 cohort: 36 organizations across four teams, loaded from a public read-only Supabase endpoint. Filter and group by team, geography, SDG, editorial topic, shared category, rating completeness, financial size, and personal decision. Compare up to four organizations and build a personal shortlist of 12 finalists with alternates and decision reasons.

Team ratings remain pending until the score workbook is supplied. Personal ratings never substitute for team results. Scores, notes, and decisions stay in your browser; export a backup to preserve them. Existing version 2 backups are supported. Shared categories and saved views are maintained by the owner in web/categories.json.

Only static website assets are stored here. Original packets, rosters, and private import records are excluded.
